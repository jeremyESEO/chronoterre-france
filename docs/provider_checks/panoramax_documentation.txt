<h1 id="panoramax"><img alt="Panoramax" src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a9/Panoramax.svg/40px-Panoramax.svg.png" /> Panoramax</h1>
<p>Panoramax is a digital resource for sharing and using 📍📷 street pictures.</p>
<p><img alt="Panoramax usage, from taking pictures, uploading, and browsing or reusing images" src="images/features.jpg" /></p>
<p>Anyone can take photographs of places visible from the public space and add them to the Panoramax database. This data is then freely accessible and reusable. It offers a similar service to StreetView, Mapillary, KartaView... but a with a completely open-source software stack, and fully managed by a growing open community.</p>
